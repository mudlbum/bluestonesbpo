## Tell us what you need

The most useful first message describes your situation rather than asking for a brochure. What
entity you have in Korea, how many people, what is due, and what is worrying you.

We will reply within one Korean business day with something specific.

> [!TIP] If you only send one thing
> Send your last VAT return or your most recent trial balance. We can tell more from either of
> those in five minutes than from an hour of general discussion.
