> [!NOTE] Review before launch
> This policy is drafted to reflect how the site is actually built — a static site with no
> account system, no third-party trackers beyond optional analytics, and a single contact form.
> It should be reviewed against your firm's actual internal data handling, and by Korean counsel,
> before the site goes live. Client engagement data is governed by your engagement terms, not by
> this page.

Bluestones BPO ("we") operates this website. This policy explains what personal information we
collect through it and what we do with it. It is written to comply with the Republic of Korea's
**Personal Information Protection Act (PIPA)**, and — because many of our visitors are in Europe
— with the GDPR where it applies.

## What we collect

**Information you send us.** If you use the contact form or email us, we receive what you choose
to provide: your name, company, work email address, the country of your head office, the service
you are enquiring about, and your message. We ask for nothing else and we do not require an
account.

**Analytics, only if you consent.** If analytics is enabled and you accept it in the cookie
banner, Google Analytics 4 records aggregate usage — pages viewed, approximate region, device
type, and how you arrived. IP addresses are anonymised. If you decline, or if you never respond
to the banner, no analytics cookies are set and no analytics data is collected. Consent Mode is
configured to deny by default before any tag loads.

**Server logs.** This site is served as static files from GitHub Pages. GitHub processes request
data, including IP addresses, as part of delivering the site. See GitHub's own privacy
statement for that processing.

**What we do not collect:** payment details, identity documents, marketing profiles bought from
third parties, or any special category data. We do not run advertising trackers, social media
pixels, or session recording.

## Why we use it

| Purpose | Legal basis (GDPR) | Basis (PIPA) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Replying to your enquiry | Legitimate interest / steps prior to a contract | Consent given by submitting the form |
| Understanding site usage | Consent | Consent |
| Meeting our own legal and record-keeping obligations | Legal obligation | Legal obligation |

We do **not** use enquiry data for marketing lists, and we do not sell or rent personal
information to anyone, ever.

## How long we keep it

- **Enquiries that do not become engagements:** deleted within 12 months.
- **Enquiries that become engagements:** retained under our engagement terms and the retention
  periods Korean law imposes on accounting and tax records.
- **Analytics data:** retained for the period configured in Google Analytics, and in aggregate
  form only.

## Who else sees it

Only the people at Bluestones BPO who need to answer you. We use third-party services to operate
the site — a static host, an email provider, and a form-processing service — and those providers
process data on our instructions. We do not transfer personal information to any other third
party for their own purposes.

Because our providers may operate outside Korea, replying to your enquiry may involve processing
your information overseas. Where required, that transfer is made on the lawful basis applicable
to it.

## Your rights

Under PIPA and, where applicable, the GDPR, you may ask us to:

- tell you what personal information we hold about you
- correct anything inaccurate
- delete it
- stop processing it
- provide it in a portable form
- withdraw consent you previously gave

Email **info@bluestonesbpo.co.kr** and we will respond within the period the applicable law
requires. We will not ask you to justify the request.

You also have the right to complain to a supervisory authority: in Korea, the **Personal
Information Protection Commission**; in the EU or UK, your national data protection authority.

## Cookies

This site sets **no cookies at all** unless you accept analytics in the banner. Details are in
the [cookie policy](/cookie-policy/). Your choice is stored in your browser's local storage, not
in a cookie, and you can clear it at any time through your browser settings.

## Security

The site is served over HTTPS. It has no database, no login, and no user-generated content,
which removes most of the attack surface a marketing site normally carries. Enquiry data is
handled inside our normal firm systems, which are subject to the confidentiality obligations we
owe every client.

## Changes

If we change this policy we will change the "last updated" date at the top of this page. Material
changes will be flagged on the page itself rather than made silently.
